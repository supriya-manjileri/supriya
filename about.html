About Our College

NMSM Government College, Kalpetta is one of the leading government colleges in Wayanad district. It provides quality higher education in Arts, Science and Commerce.

The college focuses on academic excellence, research, innovation, cultural activities and overall personality development.

Vision

To become a centre of excellence by providing quality education, promoting research and developing socially responsible citizens.

Mission

  • Provide quality education.
  • Develop leadership skills.
  • Encourage research and innovation.
  • Promote social responsibility.

Quick Facts

  • Government College
  • Located in Kalpetta, Wayanad
  • UG & PG Courses
  • NAAC Accredited
